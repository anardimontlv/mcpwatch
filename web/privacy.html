Who we are

Website

No cookies, analytics or trackers. Our host Vercel keeps request logs (IP address, user agent, URL, time) for a limited period for security and operations. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.

CLI, GitHub Action and MCP server

They run on your machine or CI runner. Your MCP configuration is never uploaded. They only download public index files from mcpwatch.io.

The public index

We build it from the official MCP registry and from read-only checks of public endpoints (details). Server names can include a maintainer's username. We process this under our legitimate interest in informing users about the security of tools they install. You can object: we will remove your server's page (anonymous aggregate statistics remain) and stop probing it.

Watchlist (free beta) and paid plans

If you sign up to watch servers, we store your email address, the list of servers you watch, timestamps and, if you connect Slack, the Slack webhook URL you give us (used only to post your digest) (lawful basis: performance of our contract with you). Your MCP configuration is never uploaded: the watch page reads it in your browser and sends only the public names of the servers you choose. Sign-ups that are not confirmed through the emailed link are deleted after 7 days. Every email we send contains a link that deletes your account and all related data immediately; links are stored only as one-way hashes.

When paid plans launch, payment is handled by our Merchant of Record as an independent controller; we will never see card details.

Who processes data for us

Vercel (hosting), Neon (database for watchlists), Resend (sending watchlist emails), Slack (only if you connect it), GitHub (code hosting and index builds) and, once paid plans launch, our payment provider. Some are in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Israel has an EU adequacy decision. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent where consent applies. Email hello@mcpwatch.io. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (EU/UK) or to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

Security

Access to personal data is limited to the operator, protected with two-factor authentication, and encrypted in transit and at rest.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date and email paid users about material changes.
