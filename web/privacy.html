Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-22.

Website

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party trackers. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and user agent) for security and operations.

CLI and GitHub Action

The scanner runs on your machine or CI runner. Your MCP configuration is never uploaded. The lock and check commands only download public index files from mcpwatch.io.

The public index

The index is built from public data in the official MCP registry and from read-only checks of publicly listed server endpoints (an MCP handshake and a tool listing; no tool is ever called).

Paid plans

Payments are handled by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record; we do not see or store card details. To run a paid plan we store your email address, your watchlist and your alert settings, only to deliver the service. We do not sell personal data. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.
