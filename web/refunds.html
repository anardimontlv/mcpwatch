Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-11-22.

If mcpwatch isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment or of any renewal. No questions asked.

Refunds are processed by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. Request one by replying to your Dodo Payments receipt email or by email to hello@mcpwatch.io. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

After 14 days you can cancel at any time; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for and are not charged again.

Refunds are handled under the customer terms of Dodo Payments (dodopayments.com). This policy adds to your legal rights and does not replace them. If you are a consumer in Israel, you may cancel a subscription at any time by email to hello@mcpwatch.io or through your Dodo Payments receipt; cancellation takes effect within 3 business days, and for annual plans we refund the unused portion pro rata.
